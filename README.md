# LatticeUI

LatticeUI is a modern, premium Android Launcher built using the Godot Engine. It features a beautiful glassmorphic design, dynamic animations, and thin white outline icons over frosted translucent tiles.

## Store Description
*Use this description when publishing the app to Itch.io, Aptoide, or other app stores.*

LatticeUI is a visually stunning, highly responsive Android launcher that brings premium glassmorphism right to your home screen. Designed for modern aesthetics, it dynamically blurs your wallpaper behind sleek, translucent tiles to give your phone an elegant, depth-filled look. 

**Important Installation Step:**
Once installed, launch the app and **enable Usage Access** when prompted in your system settings. Usage Access can be provided from app settings as well (this is required for the launcher to display your installed apps correctly).

---

## How to Change Your Wallpaper (Important!)
Because LatticeUI uses a custom rendering engine to create beautiful glassmorphic tiles, you must follow this specific process to change your background so the blur effects sync perfectly:

1. **Start in LatticeUI Settings:** Open the app settings within LatticeUI and tap **'Set Wallpaper'**.
2. **Choose a Gallery/Wallpaper App:** A menu will pop up at the bottom of your screen. Select your preferred gallery or wallpaper app from the list to open it.
3. **Set Your Image:** Browse your images, select the one you want, and set it as your wallpaper from inside that app.
4. **Return Home Quickly:** Within a couple of seconds of setting the wallpaper, press your phone's **Home button**.
5. **Wait for the Sync:** On clicking the Home button, LatticeUI will briefly capture the image in full screen to process the new background blur. Please wait **2 seconds** for this to finish.
6. **Finish:** You can now safely close the wallpaper app. Your new background and frosted glass effects are perfectly applied!
